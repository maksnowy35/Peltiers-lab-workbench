Geometry optimizer

Sweeps the leg shape with the (calibrated) 1D model and finds the maximum-power operating point of every design. The comparison constraint is explicit, as it must be: a fixed cold base (thesis A-series), a fixed hot base, or a constant volume of thermoelectric material. Limits on the smallest leg side, the joint temperature and the material temperature mark designs that cannot be built or would not survive.
